## 2. Definitions

A **chata** is one trip or event with its own page, participants and
settlement. A **participant** is a person entered on a trip; they need not
have an account. A **user** is a person with an account. A **chata admin**
is a user with admin access to their chata. The **banker** is the
participant through whom the group's money flows.

## 5. Rules for signed-in users

A signed-in user can enter their chata's expenses, edit and delete their
own, upload receipts, and confirm or reject expenses somebody recorded on
their behalf. The rules:

- enter only real expenses with real amounts; the whole group's settlement
  depends on the numbers being right,
- record an expense "paid by somebody else" only when it really happened;
  the person concerned has to confirm it, and a false entry is a breach of
  these terms,
- upload only receipts for shared expenses. Do not upload documents with
  sensitive items (medication, health services) or files you have no rights
  to,
- by confirming an expense you affirm it matches what happened.

Content you upload remains yours. You give us a licence to store it and
show it to the chata's members, because the service cannot work otherwise;
the licence lasts until you delete the content or its retention period
runs out.

## 6. Rules for chata admins

A chata admin enters other people's data, so on top of the ordinary rules
they have these duties:

- enter only people who are actually going on the trip or signing up for

- when you enter a participant, send them a link to the privacy policy (a

  without it the participant has no way of learning they are in the

- enter children's data only with a parent's agreement,
- when a participant asks you to correct or delete their data, do it or

- do not copy participants' bank details or the "Keys and Wi-Fi" section

- when handling personal data you act under our instructions and the
  privacy policy; the admin role does not entitle you to use the data for
  anything beyond organizing the trip.

Breaching these duties is grounds for removing the admin role.

## 7. The settlement is an aid, not an arbiter

The settlement is plain arithmetic over the entered shares. We do not
guarantee that the entered amounts match reality; that is the group's
business. Who sends whom how much is an agreement between participants; we
are not a party to those payments, we hold no money, and money disputes are
for the group to settle. The settlement is neither a tax document nor
financial advice. Differences within 1 CZK count as settled.
